Meet Chetna

With over 10 years of experience helping people build healthier eating habits, Chetna is passionate about making nutrition simple, practical and sustainable.

Over the years, she has supported people in improving their lifestyle through personalized diet guidance, balanced nutrition, weight-management plans and healthier everyday food choices.

Chetna has completed several certifications in nutrition, diet and wellness, continually expanding her knowledge so she can provide better and more practical guidance to her clients.

Her approach is not about starving yourself or following impossible diets. She believes that healthy eating should fit naturally into your real life — your routine, your food preferences, your family lifestyle and your individual goals.

She focuses on creating realistic nutrition plans that people can actually follow, whether their goal is weight management, healthier eating, better energy or improving their overall lifestyle.

At Chetna Dietitian, every person's journey is different, which is why every nutrition plan is designed with individual needs and preferences in mind.

Her Philosophy

Healthy eating does not have to be complicated. Small, consistent and balanced changes can make a meaningful difference to your everyday health and lifestyle.
