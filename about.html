Meet Chetna

Helping people create a healthier relationship with food, one practical and sustainable change at a time.

More Than a Diet — It's a Lifestyle

With over 10 years of experience helping people build healthier eating habits, Chetna is passionate about making nutrition simple, practical and sustainable.

Over the years, she has supported people in improving their lifestyles through personalized diet guidance, balanced nutrition, weight-management plans and healthier everyday food choices.

Chetna has completed several certifications in nutrition, diet and wellness, continually expanding her knowledge to provide better and more practical guidance to her clients.

Chetna's philosophy: Healthy eating shouldn't mean starving yourself or following an impossible diet. It should work with your real life.

Her approach focuses on understanding each person's routine, food preferences and individual wellness goals. Instead of relying on extreme restrictions, she believes in building realistic habits that can become part of everyday life.

Whether the goal is healthier eating, weight management, better meal planning or simply developing a more balanced lifestyle, the focus is on making nutrition easier to understand and easier to follow.

At Chetna Dietitian, every person's journey is different. That's why nutrition guidance is personalized rather than using the same plan for everyone.
