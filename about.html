MY STORY

Nutrition Should Work With Your Life

With over 10 years of experience helping people build healthier eating habits, Chetna is passionate about making nutrition simple, practical and sustainable.

Over the years, she has supported people with personalized diet guidance, balanced nutrition, weight-management plans and healthier everyday food choices.

Chetna has completed several certifications in nutrition, diet and wellness, continually expanding her knowledge to provide better and more practical guidance to her clients.

Her philosophy is simple: healthy eating should not mean starving yourself, following impossible routines or giving up every food you enjoy.

Instead, nutrition should work around your lifestyle, food preferences and individual wellness goals.
