Frequently Asked Questions

Are consultations available online?

Yes. Online nutrition consultations are available, making it possible to receive guidance from wherever you are.

Do you provide vegetarian diet guidance?

Yes. Nutrition plans can be designed around vegetarian preferences as well as other suitable food preferences.

Can nutrition guidance support weight management?

Yes. A personalized nutrition approach can support sustainable weight-management habits without relying on unrealistic promises or extreme routines.

Can I get nutrition guidance if I have a medical condition?

Nutrition guidance may complement appropriate medical care for conditions such as thyroid disorders, diabetes, prediabetes or PCOS. Medical diagnosis, medication and treatment should remain under the care of your qualified healthcare professional.

Which plan should I choose?

You can explore the 7-Day Starter Plan, 30-Day Transformation Plan and 90-Day Wellness Plan, then discuss which option best suits your goals.
